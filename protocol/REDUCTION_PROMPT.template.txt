Given the paper, its official repository at the pinned revision, the selected
paper-reported target result, and the fixed resource envelope below, propose
one concrete resource-bounded reproduction protocol for human review.

Inputs:
- project ID: <PROJECT_ID>
- paper and pinned repository: <PAPER_AND_REPOSITORY>
- selected target result: <TARGET_RESULT>
- resource envelope: <RESOURCE_ENVELOPE>

Trace the selected result backward from the official evaluator to the raw
inputs. Enumerate every method-defining stage, its paper or repository
evidence, official entry point, inputs, outputs, dependencies, and completion
evidence. Preserve all such stages, their order, and the provenance of
intermediate artifacts. Preserve the task, dataset and version, language,
target metric, official evaluator, prediction parsing, and the complete target
evaluation set.

Reduce resource use only within required stages. Permitted candidates include
a deterministic subset of the same training split, fewer training or
optimization steps, fewer fixed seeds, a smaller method-compatible model, and
changes to batch size, precision, or backend. For every reduction, specify one
exact value rather than a range or menu. Record original and reduced values,
fixed seeds, selected-example IDs or a deterministic selection rule, and
hashes for selection manifests. Do not let the execution agent choose an
additional reduction.

Do not skip, replace, reorder, or bypass a method-defining stage. Released
predictions, cached metrics, or published values cannot replace execution. A
released checkpoint may be used only when explicitly assigned as the input to
a required stage. Do not reduce or substitute the target evaluation set,
metric, evaluator, task, dataset version, or language.

Estimate wall time, GPU memory, CPU memory, storage, and downloads for each
stage. If the complete method pipeline and full target evaluation cannot fit
the resource envelope, return no_feasible_protocol and identify the concrete
blocker; do not make the protocol fit by removing a required stage.

Return the proposed protocol in the GOLD_METRIC_SELECTION schema, including
pipeline_stages, data_scopes, stage_budgets, released_artifact_policy,
forbidden_flow_changes, and stage-level feasibility evidence. Do not use prior
agent outcomes when choosing the protocol. The proposal is not frozen until a
human reviewer verifies it against the paper and pinned repository.
